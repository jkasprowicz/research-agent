## What Was Added to Discussion

### 1. Age as a structural dimension

The Discussion now makes the point more explicitly that:

- age is an important structural driver of the latent geometry
- age is not the sole explanation for the cluster solution
- the stronger age-group association supports this interpretation quantitatively

### 2. Sex redistribution

The Discussion now more clearly states that:

- sex redistribution across clusters is descriptive
- it should not be interpreted causally
- it still strengthens the view that the solution is not purely arbitrary

### 3. Weak care-setting association

The Discussion now uses the final categorical result to support the interpretation that:

- the clusters are more strongly driven by laboratory-profile structure than by simple care-setting labels
- this should still be read cautiously rather than as evidence of full independence

### 4. Inflammatory-renal vs basal contrast in original units

The Discussion now adds concrete original-unit contrasts:

- PCR approximately `3.6x` higher
- creatinine approximately `1.85x` higher
- urea approximately `2.15x` higher

This strengthens the biological readability of the inflammatory-renal phenotype without adding new claims.

- causal language about sex differences
