# Study Characteristics
- study objective: Assess the impact of telehealth-supported referral management on waiting-list size and waiting time for specialized care in Brazil.
- healthcare setting: Brazilian universal health system, remote referral management across multiple localities.
- patient population: Patients on specialized-care referral waiting lists.
- sample size: Baseline median waiting-list sizes ranged from 2,961 to 12,305 cases across localities.
- study design: Before-and-after intervention study with monthly follow-up after implementation.

# Data
- data source: Project/referral-management database with aggregated waiting-list and waiting-time measures.
- variables used: Locality, time since intervention, number of cases on waiting list, waiting time, priority/risk category.
- outcomes measured: Waiting-list size and waiting time for specialized care.

# Methods
- statistical methods: General linear models assessing interaction between locality and time.
- machine learning methods: No machine-learning component.
- simulation methods: No simulation component.
- queueing methods: No formal queueing model, but directly studies referral queues and backlog behavior.
- optimization methods: Operational intervention through remotely operated referral management and telehealth support.

# Results
- main findings: Telehealth-supported referral management reduced waiting-list size consistently across localities and reduced waiting times in some contexts.
- quantitative outcomes: Waiting-list reductions after six months ranged from 54.67% to 88.97%; baseline median waiting times ranged from 159 to 241 days; two localities reduced waits by 100 and 114 days.

# Limitations
- stated limitations: Before-after design without randomized control; aggregated data and contextual differences across localities.
- implicit limitations: Shows impact of an operational intervention but does not model individual waiting-list behavior or provide predictive/explainable decision support.

# Relevance for SUS Waiting Lists
- relevance score (1-5): 5
- justification: Core SUS/referral-management evidence. It anchors the practical problem and shows that regulation interventions can change waiting-list dynamics.
