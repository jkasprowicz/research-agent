# Study Characteristics
- study objective: Estimate cost per referred case for a remotely operated referral-management system supporting secondary-care access in SUS.
- healthcare setting: Regula Mais Brasil / SUS referral management across multiple localities and specialties.
- patient population: Primary-care referrals managed remotely for specialized care.
- sample size: Not clearly reported in extracted abstract; cost analyses compare localities, specialties and time periods.
- study design: Micro-costing economic analysis using time-driven activity-based costing.

# Data
- data source: Operational process mapping, resource/time estimates and referral-management cost data.
- variables used: Resource costs, personnel time, structure costs, locality, specialty, number of referrals/cases.
- outcomes measured: Cost per referred case by locality and specialty.

# Methods
- statistical methods: Descriptive cost analysis and comparisons across localities/specialties/time.
- machine learning methods: No machine-learning component.
- simulation methods: No simulation component.
- queueing methods: Referral queue context, but no queueing-theory model.
- optimization methods: Economic evaluation to support management decisions about telehealth referral systems.

# Results
- main findings: Remote referral management had low per-case costs and variable costs by locality/specialty, supporting telehealth as a potentially efficient management strategy.
- quantitative outcomes: Cost per referred case ranged from R$5.70 to R$8.29 across localities and from R$1.85 to R$8.56 across specialties.

# Limitations
- stated limitations: Generalizability limited because data came from components of the same project; no full economic comparison with conventional systems.
- implicit limitations: Economic costing, not predictive modeling; does not directly model waiting-time reduction or prioritization quality.

- relevance score (1-5): 5
- justification: Important SUS evidence for the cost/feasibility side of referral-management decision support.
