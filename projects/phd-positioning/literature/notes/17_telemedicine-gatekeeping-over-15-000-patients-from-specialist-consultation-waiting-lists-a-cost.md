# Study Characteristics
- study objective: Evaluate cost-minimization of RegulaSUS telemedicine gatekeeping for specialist consultation waiting lists.
- healthcare setting: RegulaSUS and Ambulatory Regulation Center of Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil.
- patient population: Patients on specialist consultation waiting lists managed by telemedicine gatekeeping or usual regulation.
- sample size: 15,064 patients managed within primary care rather than referred to specialists.
- study design: Retrospective cohort cost-analysis/cost-minimization study.

# Data
- data source: RegulaSUS/Gercon operational data, process mapping and TDABC costing inputs.
- variables used: Gatekeeping pathway, costs, time expenditure, case throughput, referral outcomes and care setting.
- outcomes measured: Cost per managed case and health-system/societal savings.

# Methods
- statistical methods: Cost-minimization analysis using TDABC; comparisons with contemporaneous controls.
- machine learning methods: No machine-learning component.
- simulation methods: No simulation component.
- queueing methods: Waiting-list gatekeeping context, but no formal queueing model.
- optimization methods: Telemedicine gatekeeping as an operational resource-allocation intervention.

# Results
- main findings: RegulaSUS telemedicine gatekeeping reduced costs relative to usual procedures and avoided specialist referrals for many patients.
- quantitative outcomes: Telemedicine gatekeeping cost $85.02 versus $214.45 for usual procedures, a 60% reduction; reported societal savings of R$7,833,882.56 over 36 months.

# Limitations
- stated limitations: Assumptions were needed when precise time/cost data were unavailable; limited transparency of state gatekeeping activity and referral removal reasons.
- implicit limitations: Strong economic/management evidence but does not predict which patients will progress, drop out or require intervention.

# Relevance for SUS Waiting Lists
- relevance score (1-5): 5
- justification: Central evidence for SUS referral-management value and practical managerial impact.
