# Study Characteristics
- study objective: Assess how discrete-event simulation can evaluate the impact of uncertainty on operating-room waiting time.
- healthcare setting: Operating-room use case inspired by Hôpital Privé de La Baie, Vivalto Santé French health group.
- patient population: Elective and/or OR patients represented in a simulation model.
- sample size: Simulation case study; no fixed empirical patient sample in the extracted abstract.
- study design: Simulation methodology/case study.

# Data
- data source: Deterministic OR model plus uncertainty assumptions for durations, arrivals and care requirements.
- variables used: Activity durations, patient arrival times, care requirements, surgery schedule, OR process steps.
- outcomes measured: Patient waiting time and effect of uncertainty scenarios.

# Methods
- statistical methods: Scenario analysis of simulation outputs.
- machine learning methods: No machine-learning component.
- simulation methods: Discrete-event simulation under uncertainty.
- queueing methods: Queue/wait behavior modeled through DES, not analytical queueing.
- optimization methods: No direct optimizer; scenario assessment for planning.

# Results
- main findings: The paper proposes a 3-step DES approach for moving from deterministic OR planning to uncertainty-aware evaluation of waiting time.
- quantitative outcomes: No central numeric outcome in extracted abstract; results are scenario-based.

# Limitations
- stated limitations: Use-case inspired by one partner OR and dependent on uncertainty assumptions.
- implicit limitations: More method demonstration than validated decision-support system; not specific to SUS waiting lists.
