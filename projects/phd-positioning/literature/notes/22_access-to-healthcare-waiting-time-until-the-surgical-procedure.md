# Study Characteristics
- study objective: Evaluate time between diagnosis and elective surgery in adults with long waiting lists in Brazil and analyze determinants and quality of life.
- healthcare setting: Three hospitals in Southeast, North and South Brazil.
- patient population: Adult surgical patients with procedures from long waiting lists.
- sample size: 250 patients.
- study design: Cross-sectional multicenter study with postoperative quality-of-life follow-up.

# Data
- data source: Patient interviews and REDCap data collection; hospital schedules/procedure selection.
- variables used: Sociodemographics, region/hospital, diagnosis-to-surgery time, canceled surgery, medical history, age, WHOQOL-Bref domains.
- outcomes measured: Waiting time until surgery and quality-of-life measures after indication/surgery.

# Methods
- statistical methods: Descriptive statistics, chi-square, ANOVA/simulated chi-square, Kruskal-Wallis and mixed-effects linear regression.
- machine learning methods: No machine-learning component.
- simulation methods: No simulation component.
- queueing methods: No queueing model; access-delay epidemiological analysis.
- optimization methods: No optimization model.

# Results
- main findings: Access varied by region and waiting time was long, with longer waits associated with worse perceived impact among older/comorbid patients.
- quantitative outcomes: Mean age 51.86 years; longest mean waiting time 26.23 months in the South region; differences between institutions p=0.02.

# Limitations
- stated limitations: Excludes patients with communication/mental-health limitations and uses postoperative recruitment; regional/hospital sample may limit generalization.
- implicit limitations: Relevant for consequences of waiting, but less useful for computational decision support without longitudinal queue event data.

# Relevance for SUS Waiting Lists
- relevance score (1-5): 4
- justification: Good Brazilian evidence on long waits and patient-level burden; supports impact justification more than method design.
