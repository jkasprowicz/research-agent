- title: Implementation and evaluation of a multidisciplinary training course in waiting list management
- authors: Rosa Ladi Lisboa; Adriana Aparecida Paz
- year: 2025
- country: Brazil

# Study Characteristics
- study objective: Implement and evaluate satisfaction and knowledge retention in a multidisciplinary waiting-list management course.
- healthcare setting: Virtual learning environment for healthcare professionals involved in waiting-list management in Brazil.
- patient population: Healthcare professionals enrolled in the course.
- sample size: 768 enrolled; 429 accessed the VLE; 255 completed the course.
- study design: Methodological educational-technology study using two ADDIE phases.

# Data
- data source: Questionnaires and VLE-generated reports.
- variables used: Course access/completion, satisfaction, presentation/content/applicability ratings, knowledge retention.
- outcomes measured: Satisfaction and knowledge retention.

# Methods
- statistical methods: Descriptive statistics and Wilcoxon test (p<0.05).
- machine learning methods: No machine-learning component.
- simulation methods: No simulation component.
- queueing methods: No queueing model; management capacity-building context.
- optimization methods: Training intervention to improve waiting-list management practice.

# Results
- main findings: The course had high satisfaction and significant knowledge retention among participants who completed it.
- quantitative outcomes: 429/768 accessed the VLE; 255/429 completed; knowledge retention z=-8.09, p<0.001.

# Limitations
- stated limitations: Course participation/completion attrition and educational context may limit generalization to operational outcomes.
- implicit limitations: Does not measure queue performance after training; relevant to implementation readiness more than computational method.
