# Study Characteristics
- study objective: Argue for and describe a multidisciplinary task-force model to reduce pediatric ENT surgical backlogs in Brazilian public healthcare.
- healthcare setting: Tertiary university hospital and pediatric ENT surgical campaigns in Brazil.
- patient population: Pediatric patients awaiting low-complexity ENT surgeries such as adenotonsillectomy and tympanostomy tube placement.
- sample size: Editorial reports ten campaigns over two years treating 200 pediatric patients from the waiting list.
- study design: Editorial/commentary with local experience description.

# Data
- data source: Authors’ task-force experience and cited public-health context.
- variables used: Weekend scheduling, multidisciplinary team, academic integration, partnerships, patient selection and safety protocols.
- outcomes measured: Number of patients treated, operational model feasibility and educational/social benefits.

# Results
- main findings: Coordinated weekend campaigns and partnerships can reduce pediatric ENT backlogs locally without major new technology.
- quantitative outcomes: Ten campaigns; 200 pediatric patients treated; national elective surgeries dropped >30% between 2020 and 2021 according to cited context.

# Limitations

- implicit limitations: Useful for practical strategies and impact narrative, but not a computational study.
